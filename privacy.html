NestLog Privacy Policy

NestLog – Tracker & Journal · Effective October 1, 2026

The short version: NestLog doesn't collect your data. What you log stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. We can't see it.

  • No NestLog account, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • No third-party code that collects data.
  • We never sell or share your information, because we never receive it.

What you enter in NestLog

NestLog stores what you choose to log about your baby: their name and date of birth, feedings, diaper changes, sleep, medicines, and journal entries with any photos you add. This information is saved on your device.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, NestLog keeps your data in sync across your devices using Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), stored in your own private iCloud account. Apple stores it under the Apple Privacy Policy. The developer of NestLog has no access to your iCloud data.

NestLog stores your log entries in CloudKit's encrypted fields, which are end-to-end encrypted: the keys live in your iCloud Keychain, and neither Apple nor the developer can read them. Photos are encrypted by Apple in transit and on its servers, and are end-to-end encrypted if you turn on Advanced Data Protection for iCloud.

If you aren't signed in to iCloud, your data stays only on your device.

Family Sharing

If you invite someone to share your NestLog data, they can see and edit everything you've logged, and you can see what they add. They join with their own Apple ID through iCloud sharing, and you choose who to invite. You can stop sharing at any time in Settings → Family Sharing. People you shared with keep a copy of what was shared before you stopped.

To let shared data keep syncing, the person who started sharing has their NestLog Premium status (the date their subscription runs until) stored with the shared data, where the people they invited can see it.

Subscriptions

NestLog Premium is purchased through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and the developer never receives your payment details. NestLog checks whether your subscription is active on your device, using Apple's StoreKit. Apple shares sales reports with the developer that don't identify you.

Notifications

Feed and medicine reminders are scheduled on your device. NestLog also receives silent notifications from Apple's iCloud servers that tell it when shared data has changed. Neither involves sending your information to the developer.

Crash reports

NestLog contains no analytics or crash reporting code. If you've chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide the developer with crash reports and usage statistics that don't identify you. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Children's privacy

NestLog is made for parents and caregivers, not for children to use. Information about your child is entered by you, and is handled as described above: it stays on your devices and in your iCloud account.

Deleting your data

  • Delete individual entries in the app at any time.
  • Deleting NestLog removes the data stored on that device.
  • To delete NestLog's data from iCloud, open Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage (or Manage Storage) → NestLog, and delete its data.
  • To stop sharing, use Settings → Family Sharing in NestLog.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If NestLog ever starts collecting information, this policy will be updated before it does.
