NestLog – Tracker & Journal

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Email nestlog.support@proton.me and include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Sharing with a partner

In NestLog, open Settings → Family Sharing → Invite Partner and send the invite in Messages or Mail. Your partner taps the link on an iPhone signed in to iCloud and NestLog opens with your shared data. Inviting needs NestLog Premium; joining is free.

Entries aren't syncing

  • Make sure both devices are signed in to iCloud.
  • Check that both devices are online, then open NestLog on each.
  • If Settings → Family Sharing says sharing is paused, the family's Premium subscription has ended. Entries are kept on the device and sync again once it's renewed.

Subscriptions

NestLog Premium is managed by Apple. To cancel or change it, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or Settings → Premium → Manage Subscription in NestLog. To restore a purchase on a new device, open the Premium screen and tap Restore Subscription.

Your data

NestLog has no accounts and doesn't collect your data. Read the privacy policy.
